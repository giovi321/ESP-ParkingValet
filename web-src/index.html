Detection sensitivity

Watch each cell's live Edge and Baseline. Absolute: set threshold between empty-asphalt edge and parked-car edge. Relative (best for sun vs shade): a cell trips when its Edge rises the delta above its own auto-tracked empty Baseline. Raise Stable frames to ride out moving shadows.
Snaps the empty Baseline to the current view for every cell. Use only when the whole curb is empty. To re-baseline a single cell, right-click its chip (or its polygon) in the Cells card on the Live tab. Left-clicking a chip toggles its dead-zone mask.

Curb geometry & counting

Car pitch is used with reliable_range_m to estimate how many cars can fit. Interior / End clearance accounts for driveways or no-stop zones. Pitch auto-learning adjusts the pitch estimate as the system observes real parked cars.

Calibration helpers

Park the curb bumper-to-bumper, enter the car count N, click Calibrate — sets Car pitch = total strip length ÷ N and saves. This is the most accurate way to calibrate the pitch estimate. Calibrating from a full street also turns off pitch auto-learning so it cannot override your explicit value.

Connectivity watchdog

      
      
      
      
      

      
If a WiFi join fails the device falls back to this AP for setup. With retry set, it re-attempts the saved WiFi every N minutes and switches back to STA once it reconnects — staying in AP (and looping) until then; this hotspot is briefly unreachable during each attempt. Auto-reboot reboots after the device has been offline this long, as a last-resort recovery — set retry shorter than auto-reboot for it to run first. Either or both; 0 disables.
